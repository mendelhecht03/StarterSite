Alter Rebbe's Ruling (שו"ע אדמו"ר הזקן או"ח צ"ד):
One must face toward the Beit HaMikdash — the Temple Mount in Jerusalem — not merely "east." This compass calculates the precise great-circle bearing to the Kodesh HaKodashim (31.7781°N, 35.2358°E) from your exact location, as required by the Alter Rebbe's ruling that the direction be geographically accurate and not approximate.
